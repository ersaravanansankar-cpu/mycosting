BEHIND THE RATE BANK

Better cost decisions need context.

The goal of MyCosting is not simply to publish a number. A useful rate should progressively retain its cost basis, specification, location, source and effective date.

01

Project Cost Data

BOQ and project references can provide cost-management benchmarks.

02

Procurement Data

Supplier and procurement references can support market-rate comparisons.

03

Rate Analysis

Material, labour, equipment and other components can support detailed build-ups.

04

Specification

Rates become more useful when users can understand what quality and scope they represent.

Important: MyCosting rate references should be treated as indicative cost information unless a rate is explicitly supported by a current verified quotation or other identified source. Final project prices vary with scope, specification, location, quantity, procurement conditions and project requirements.
